# Prompt 2 — your taste

Pick 3–5 reels whose editing you like and replace the links.

```
Here are reels whose editing I like:
[link 1] [link 2] [link 3]
Download them with yt-dlp into references.
Study each frame by frame and by ear:
pacing, subtitles, colors, music, first 3 s.
Take inspiration, but don't copy.
Write my own style to style.md.
```

The key line is **"Take inspiration, but don't copy."** It's what keeps your
videos from looking like everyone else's.
